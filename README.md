One joke about office life, one line, under 200 characters — written to
`/output/entry.txt` by a stdlib-only Python program.

> Someone hit reply-all to ask everyone to stop replying all. Forty-seven people have replied all to agree.

The joke turns on the reply-all paradox: the plea to stop replying all is
itself answered by reply-all, so the complaint becomes the offence.
